Privacy policy

Creator Desk uses YouTube API Services. Google’s handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What the app accesses

When the owner connects a YouTube channel, the app obtains permission to identify that channel, read its videos and performance data, and upload and schedule videos. This can include unpublished videos, titles, descriptions, timestamps, thumbnails, and engagement counts. The app saves OAuth credentials and cached results locally under the owner’s macOS user account.

How the app uses data

Creator Desk uses this data to show the owner’s channels, report publishing status, and avoid duplicate uploads. The factory sends the selected video and its metadata to YouTube when the owner enables scheduling. Creator Desk does not sell Google data or send it to its image or animation providers. Google data is used in accordance with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Access and deletion

You can revoke access in Google’s security settings. The owner can remove local saved connection records and cached YouTube data on the Mac. For assistance with access or deletion, contact the owner through the support email shown in the Creator Desk Google authorization screen.
